ANSWER DELIVERED (full text in outputs/answer.md)
  `--prune <N>` means "keep only the N most recent backups" (src/backup.js:157), but in
  `doflow remove` it does nothing: it parses fine, and cmdRemove never reads it. The only two
  consumers are cmdInstall (bin/doflow.js:1030) and cmdUpdate (:1112), each running right after that
  run's own backup is written. Plus two caveats: `parseInt(val,10) || 0` silently turns a
  non-numeric --prune into 0 (unlike --days, which exits 2 with a message), and --prune is
  documented only in the --help block, not in docs/ or README.md.

  Length: one direct answer with locators. No decomposition, no task-class proposal, no evidence
  batch, no skill chain -- which is what this eval is checking for.
