# Deep Web Research Reference

Methodology for multi-hop evidence collection and cited reporting:

## Research Process
1. **Query Decomposition**: Break high-level queries into independent and sequenced search vectors.
2. **Batch Retrieval**: Execute independent searches in parallel; follow entity hops.
3. **Claim & Source Tracking**: Link every claim directly to a cited source URL with confidence ratings.
4. **Report Output**: Save detailed findings with an Executive Summary and Citation Table to `agent-docs/research/[topic]_[timestamp].md`.

## Behavioral Posture
For deep investigation protocols and source confidence thresholds, consult `modes/MODE_DeepResearch.md` and `references/RESEARCH_CONFIG.md`.
